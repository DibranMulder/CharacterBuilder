# Lineage Forge

Character terminology follows the playable peoples in the game-design records.
These are working names, not a declaration that all art direction is final.

## Language

**Lineage**:
A playable people's body, cultural origin, visual heritage, and Homeland,
independent of their learned Combat Class.
_Avoid_: Race, class (when referring to a people)

**Allegiance**:
A Hero's permanent Light or Dark affiliation, inherited from their Lineage;
it is not a measure of personal morality and cannot be switched.

**Adventure Level**:
A Hero's 1–120 adventure progression, governing encounter scale, equipment
requirements, and Class Talent progression independently of Disciplines.

**Class Talent Point**:
A Hero-specific point for a Class Talent, earned every ten Adventure Levels
for a maximum of twelve at level 120.

**Discipline**:
One of twelve independently trained 1–99 skills expressing a Hero's breadth;
their aggregate Overall Level does not award Class Talent Points.

**Map**:
The player-facing name for one playable Zone, distinct from a Homeland or
Region containing multiple Maps.

**Recovery Anchor**:
A safe location within a Map for respawn or correction of an invalid position;
it is not a destination in another Map.

**NPC Shop**:
A fixed-price source of default weapons, basic armor, potions, and routine
supplies, distinct from player trading.

**Exchange**:
The shared player market for tradable goods, including special drops and
upgraded equipment, with player-set prices and escrowed ownership.

**Tidekin**:
The Light-Allegiance amphibious people of the Tidekin Sea.
_Avoid_: Bogkin

**Humans**:
The Light-Allegiance people of the Open Lands; an individual is a Human.

**Grove Centaurs**:
The Light-Allegiance equine forest people of the Elder Forests.
_Avoid_: Centaurs (as the full Lineage name)

**Aeralith**:
The Light-Allegiance wind-shaped people of the Sky Reaches.
_Avoid_: Fae

**Crag Trolls**:
The Dark-Allegiance mountain clans of the Broken Mountains.
_Avoid_: Frost Trolls

**Deep Goblins**:
The Dark-Allegiance tunnel societies of the Underdeep.
_Avoid_: Goblins (as the full Lineage name)

**Sunscour**:
The working name for the Dark-Allegiance desert people of the Ember Desert.
The Sunscour Legion is a military organization within their culture.
_Avoid_: Duneborn, Sunscour Legion (as the Lineage name)

**Rimeborn**:
The Dark-Allegiance cold-adapted people of the Ice Lands.
_Avoid_: Frostlings
