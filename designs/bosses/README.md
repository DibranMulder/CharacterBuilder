distinct boss for every stronghold. Nimrod is an additional tower encounter.

## Approved visual direction

Use a side-on gameplay camera, small readable party sprites, horizontal
platforms, navy/gold interface, local attack effects and visible ground warnings.
The dark-lineage party attacks the centaur tree in the approved reference.
Other parties in the generated sheets are illustrative, not faction rules.

## Generation brief

The final prompt set requested 2D side-scrolling gameplay mockups matching the
approved tree screenshot: painted cutout characters, orthographic camera,
functional boss HUD, readable platforms and localized VFX. Each stronghold uses
its own environment: forest ruins; sandstone fortress; underground goblin
tunnels; ice palace; flooded marsh causeway; ruined throne hall; mountaintop
sanctuary; carved mountain fortress. Nimrod uses a blue-and-gold Babylonian
tower terrace, lion-headed mace and Royal Decree's marked falling masonry.

The tree and Nimrod are individual scenes. The other seven bosses are grouped
on two review sheets. Earlier cinematic tree artwork was superseded by the
approved gameplay framing and is not part of this gallery.
