# Tidekin hometown and encounter update — 2026-09-15

## Delivered

- The 17 peaceful town/citadel/shrine maps now include the authored Tidekin contacts and shared hometown roster: 25 residents, including six class trainers, vendors, ferrymaster, innkeeper, quartermaster, broker, sentries and the bonded guardian. All are noncombat characters. Stores use existing purchase/sale transactions; skills and Hero overview use the shared panels.
- Sera → Shallows samples → Mero → Coru → Amaya → Nave repairs → ordered Reliquary conduits → lens installation → Sera forms the local investigation. Shrine authorization still requires Light allegiance. Landing chores cannot bypass it. Dark visitors receive public water-care guidance.
- Separate, atomic Tidekin checkpoints preserve inventory, progression, current map/position, discovery, encounter populations/rewards, partial repairs, the unique lens and quest payout. The shared test harness never reads or writes player saves.
- All 39 widths doubled to 3,600–6,400 units. Town walking areas are open; remaining platform routes retain ordinary-jump reachability. Background scale is independent of map width, with distant parallax and atmospheric contrast. NPC rigs render once into map-lifetime textures; their visible sprites do not rebuild meshes each frame.
- Level-appropriate Common monsters take roughly 4–6 reference hits. Five-level cohorts stay accessible; resistance grows for larger gaps. Monsters 25+ levels above the Hero resist damage and control, including lineage skills; their attacks deal substantially increased damage. A level-1 Hero cannot beat a level-30 monster.
- Filled yellow hitboxes are replaced by thin ground brackets, melee arcs and coral/cyan ranged effects. Creature body anticipation and the bright gold portal design remain.

Rendered captures: `artifacts/tidekin_updated_*.png`, generated by `tools/capture_tidekin_town.gd`. Checked landing, market, training yard, guardian, combat and shrine presentation. The NPC dialogue panel is also captured for inspection.

Matched rendering runs used Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility/OpenGL on Apple M1 at 1152×648. Timed runs were sequential. Benchmark-only VSync settings stay in the harness.

## Prototype boundaries

The full regional design document remains a broader production target: tides are cosmetic, the Exchange and durable bank are information-only services, and the Regent does not yet have the complete proposed encounter choreography. Regional border-road labels describe the world geography; this update does not implement the neighboring homelands. These are not claims that the entire DESIGN-0023 production specification is finished.
